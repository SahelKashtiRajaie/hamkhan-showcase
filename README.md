# Hamkhan Integrated Management & Operations System

An integrated operational management platform designed and developed to streamline educational programs, instructor coordination, content planning, customer relationship management, sales operations, attendance tracking, internal meetings, and organizational workflows.

The system was developed using **Google Apps Script** and **Google Workspace**, with integrations for Google Sheets, Google Drive, Google Meet, Telegram Bot API, and AI-assisted workflows.

---

## Project Overview

Hamkhan is a unified web application that brings multiple operational functions together in one centralized environment.

Instead of relying on disconnected spreadsheets, messaging channels, manual follow-ups, and separate management tools, the platform connects key organizational processes through structured workflows, centralized data, dashboards, and automated notifications.

## Key Modules & Features

### 1. Centralized Access Hub
- Centralized directory of organizational tools, platforms, and resources.
- Search, categorization, editing, and archiving of links.
- Access information, authorized users, review frequency, and importance indicators.

### 2. Instructor Management
- Instructor registration and profile management.
- Reusable instructor profiles.
- Structured course and event brief submission.
- Venue selection and venue database management.
- Telegram notifications for new submissions and updates.

### 3. Educational Program & Course Calendar
- Jalali calendar for course and session scheduling.
- Color-coded event categories.
- Individual session tracking.
- Identification of upcoming final sessions and instructor renewal milestones.
- Cancellation tracking and automatic replacement-session scheduling.
- Search and event-type filtering.

### 4. Sequential Brief Review
- Consolidated view of submitted instructor proposals.
- Sequential review of course and event briefs.
- Centralized comparison and planning.

### 5. Course Information & Publication
- Management of finalized course information.
- Poster, website description, registration link, and Telegram group link management.
- Automatic poster organization in Google Drive.
- Google Meet link generation when required.
- Telegram notifications for important publication updates.

### 6. Session Operations & Reporting
- Completed and canceled session tracking.
- Cancellation reasons and operational notes.
- Session reports and challenges.
- Visual and video report uploads.
- AI-assisted report drafting and editing.
- Telegram reporting with categorized hashtags.
- Edit history notifications.

### 7. Social Media & Content Management
- Content planning for Telegram, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
- Content format, topic, objective, and publication date tracking.
- Jalali content calendar.
- Platform-specific indicators and visual distinctions.
- Content performance recording and analytics.
- Monthly comparisons, top-performing content, format analysis, and hashtag performance.

### 8. Course Analytics Dashboard
- Overview of courses and their operational status.
- Analysis by genre, format, pricing, and delivery type.
- Planned, completed, and canceled session statistics.
- Instructor-level performance and session records.
- Cancellation analysis and operational reporting.

### 9. CRM & Lead Management
- Lead registration and centralized tracking.
- Managerial lead assignment.
- Follow-up status management.
- Lead conversion and cancellation metrics.
- Individual and management dashboards.
- Task assignment for lead follow-ups.
- Telegram notifications for new leads and assigned tasks.

### 10. Financial & Sales Management
- Revenue analysis by course and instructor.
- Subscription and registration type tracking.
- Sales team performance dashboard.
- Manual participant registration when website registration is unavailable.
- Payment receipt uploads and Google Drive storage.
- Integration of manual registrations into CRM records.

### 11. Attendance & Participant Management
- Course-based attendance registration.
- Participant notes and attendance history.
- Subscription type and remaining capacity tracking.
- Refund status management.

### 12. Internal Meetings & Task Management
- Internal meeting and work-session calendar.
- Recurring meetings and participant management.
- Automatic Google Meet link generation.
- Calendar invitations via email.
- Meeting minutes recording.
- AI-assisted meeting minutes processing.
- Extraction of decisions, suggestions, and actionable tasks.
- Task assignment, deadlines, priorities, and status tracking.
- Subtask management and progress dashboards.

### 13. Telegram Automation & Daily Reporting
- Automated notifications for operational events.
- Categorized Telegram messages with hashtags.
- Daily overview of meetings and course sales.
- Registration analysis by sales channel and subscription type.
- Extraction of registration information from website emails.
- Identification of courses currently open for registration.
